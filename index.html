§ 05 · Our story

Saved from the dust

A collective of local historians and audio engineers opened a small shop in Woodstock in 2018, after watching master tapes rot in garages and church halls.

Since then we have digitised more than five thousand tracks, and we still press and dub the physical copies because holding the record is part of the history. Every item in the crate comes with its provenance: who played on it, where it was cut, and how it reached us.

We source unreleased demos, bootlegs and official pressings that never saw a proper release, and we ship them anywhere in the world.

Keep it spinning

SA Sound Archive is a fictional store. I rebuilt it from a second-year Wix project to show what the same brief looks like with real craft. The products, people and event are invented; the music history is real. Every sound is synthesised in the browser with the Web Audio API.
